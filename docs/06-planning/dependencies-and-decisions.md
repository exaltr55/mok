# 6.3 Dependencies · 6.8 Open Questions

> Source: §6.3 + §6.8 of the Mokshly YouSourceful Platform Design Document.

---

## Critical Dependencies

- **Content production** — full 5S + 7 Practices content in audio/video format pre-launch.
- **Brand design system finalized.**
- **Anchor enterprise customer** committed by Week 12.
- **Anchor donor** for Mokshly Access committed by Week 12.
- **Insurance anchor partner** committed by Week 14.
- **Live Audio provider decision** by Week 2.

---

## Locked Pricing Decisions

1. **Mokshly Commons:** $100/year flat annual subscription, **no monthly option**.
2. **Enterprise:** per-employee annual pricing (specific rate TBD by sales).
3. **Mokshly Access:** scholarship-funded (no payment from recipient).
4. **Trial:** 30-day free trial standard. **60-day Early Practitioner offer at launch (time-limited).**
5. **Refund policy:** no refunds once annual subscription begins; pause-and-resume available (90 days max, doesn't extend term).

---

## Open Decisions Requiring Leadership Input

2. Insurance data-sharing contract template.
3. Length of "Early Practitioner" 60-day trial promotion (first 6 months? first N members?).
4. Anchor donor commitment date and grant size.
5. Data residency — EU option in MVP or v1.1?
6. Mokshly Access application review process — manual vs. partial automation in MVP.
7. Member-Partner v1.1 — which anchor partner do we target first?

---

## Technical Decisions

1. **Cloud provider:** AWS recommended.
2. **Live Audio:** LiveKit + one alternative in Week 2.
3. **CMS:** Sanity recommended.
4. **Analytics warehouse:** BigQuery for MVP.
5. **Observability:** Datadog for MVP.
6. **Email service:** Resend recommended for newsletter and transactional.
7. **Billing:** Stripe Billing + Stripe Invoicing + Stripe Tax for MVP.
