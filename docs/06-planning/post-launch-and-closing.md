## 6.7 Post-Launch Evolution (First 12 Months)

### Months 1–3 — Stabilize and Listen
- No new features beyond bug fixes.
- Weekly cohort health reviews.
- Monthly user research.
- Iterate nudge timing and content.
- First enterprise renewal conversations.

### Months 4–6 — v1.1 Expansion
- AI personalization (Tier 4 consent).
- Mokshly Tools MVP — constitutional assessment.
- Native iOS and Android apps.
- Spanish language.

### Months 7–9 — v1.2 Depth
- Advanced cohort features — async threading, cohort-curated resources.
- Vedic chart integration for Tools.
- Hindi language.
- Wearable integration prototypes.

### Months 10–12 — v2 Commerce
- Mokshly Shop launched.
- Deeper partner integration.
- SOC 2 Type 2 audit.
- ISO 27001 preparation.

## 6.9 Closing Statement

This design document represents the foundation for Mokshly's YouSourceful platform MVP. **It embodies a clear product philosophy: technology in service of human flourishing, not the other way around.**

The decisions encoded here — **consistency over completion, minimum viable engagement, self-competition over social comparison, privacy as a product feature, cohort intimacy over community scale, user-centric memberships over tenant-owned users** — are unusual in the SaaS landscape. They are also what will allow Mokshly to build something meaningful in a crowded market.

The 3 to 4 month MVP timeline is ambitious but achievable with a focused team, clear scope, and **leadership commitment to preserving these principles when pressure to dilute them arises.**

Every feature decision, every sprint commitment, every tradeoff made during implementation should be tested against the two foundational truths: **the practice is a doorway not a destination, and the player keeps their own score.** When in doubt, we choose the option that honors these truths.

This document is version 1.1 and will continue to evolve as we learn from building. It should be treated as a living foundation, not a finished blueprint.
