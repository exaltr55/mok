# Mokshly Connect (Cohort System)

> Source: §2.3.4 of the Mokshly YouSourceful Platform Design Document.

---

## Daily Cohort View (3-minute cap)

- See which cohort members have practiced today (**indicator only**).
- Send one-tap acknowledgment — "I see you" signal, no messaging.
- View current week's prompt as reminder.
- **3-minute daily timer** — view gently closes after.

---

## Weekly Connect (15-minute Live Audio or Async)

Detailed flow in [User Flows → Flow 3](../01-product/user-flows.md#flow-3--weekly-cohort-connect-flow).

- **Live Audio:** Mokshly-hosted room, one-tap join, structured app-driven agenda.
- **Async:** 24-hour window for responses, then 24 hours for acknowledgments.

---

## Cohort Profile

- Members by first name and single-sentence intention.
- Time zone, preferred meeting time, career stage (if shared).
- **No photos required** — optional avatar or initials.
- Cohort formation date, current cycle week.

---

## Cohort Rules (Always Visible)

1. We speak from our own experience. We don't give advice.
2. We listen without fixing or responding.
3. We keep what's shared here confidential.
4. We honor the timer. Our time is a gift to each other.
5. We show up as we are.

---

## Re-commitment Moment

- At **week 13** of each cycle: "Stay with this cohort for another cycle, or rotate?"
- Majority choice governs.
- Rotating members join next formation wave.
