# Mokshly Weekly Newsletter

> Source: §2.3.7 of the Mokshly YouSourceful Platform Design Document.

## Purpose

Mokshly Weekly is the **editorial voice of Mokshly speaking to the community**. It is a thoughtful, calm, weekly reflection that respects the user's time and life.

---

## Format — 5 Sections

1. **Section 1 — A Word for the Week.** A short reflection from the content team (2–3 paragraphs). The heart of the newsletter.
2. **Section 2 — This Week's Cohort Prompt.** The same prompt cohorts will reflect on, shared with the wider community.
3. **Section 3 — A Practice in Focus.** One of the 7 Practices, briefly explored — a tip, refinement, or perspective shift. Rotates weekly.
4. **Section 4 — A Note from a Practitioner (optional, opt-in).** A short anonymous reflection from a practitioner who chose to share. 2–3 sentences.
5. **Section 5 — Mokshly Notes (brief).** Platform updates, upcoming community moments. Kept short — never the centerpiece.

---

## Cadence and Timing

- **Frequency:** Weekly only. **No daily, no extras.**
- **Delivery:** Friday afternoon (in user's local time zone).
- **Length:** 3–4 minute read.

---

## Subscription Model

- **Required for first 4 weeks** (part of the onboarding arc). Users receive the first 4 newsletters automatically.
- After week 4, **easy one-tap unsubscribe** in newsletter and Settings.
- Re-subscribe anytime.

---

## Voice and Anti-Patterns

- Calm, reflective, **never promotional**.
- No subject lines that imply urgency or FOMO.
- No "you missed X this week" framing.
- No promotional content for Mokshly's services beyond Mokshly Notes.
- No re-engagement nudges disguised as newsletter content.
- **Tracking limited to open rate and unsubscribe rate.**

---

## Production

- Owned by **Content Curator** team.
- Editorial cadence: 1 week ahead, 4-week content plan rolling.
- Quality bar: Every piece read aloud before sending.
