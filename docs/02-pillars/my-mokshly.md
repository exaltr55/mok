# My Mokshly (Personal Dashboard)

> Source: §2.3.3 of the Mokshly YouSourceful Platform Design Document.

## Purpose

My Mokshly is the user's **private sanctuary**.

---

## Components

### Journal (I M Writing)

- One entry per day maximum.
- Three style options: **Expressive, Reflective, Gratitude.**
- **20-minute in-session cap.**
- Search available; no tagging.
- Export to plain text, always available.

### Aligning Reflections

- **Weekly** "Did my choices feel aligned?" quick reflection.
- **Quarterly** "What is working? What is no longer working? What is this phase asking?" deeper reflection.

### Practice History

- Calendar-style view showing **days practiced** (not which practices, not duration).
- Soft neutral tones. Practiced days marked with a quiet dot.
- No graphs, no statistics beyond "days practiced in last 30 days."

### Mokshly Consistency Index (MCI)

- Single number on the dashboard.
- Weekly change indicator.
- Milestone label when applicable.
- Tap for plain-language explanation and 90-day MCI line (**no peer comparison**).

See [MCI specification](../03-systems/mci.md).

### Memberships View

- The user's primary membership and any affiliations.
- Clear indication of which tenant(s) receive aggregate data (with consent).
- **One-tap revocation** of any tenant's aggregate consent.

### Preferences & Settings

- Notification controls, quiet hours.
- **Cohort preferences** — request new cohort, change meeting time, opt-out of cohort.
- **Consent management** — revoke any tenant's aggregate sharing, delete account.
- **Data export** — full personal data export in standard format.
- **Phase** — current phase display and request transition.
