# Mokshly Learn (5S Framework)

> Source: §2.3.1 of the Mokshly YouSourceful Platform Design Document.

## Purpose

Mokshly Learn delivers the conceptual foundation that makes the 7 Practices meaningful rather than mechanical.

## Content

- Welcome to YouSourceful.
- **Source (S1)** — Who you truly are.
- **Seed (S2)** — Thought as pattern.
- **Soil (S3)** — Patterns meeting the world.
- **Seasons (S4)** — Time, unfolding, alignment.
- **Sowing (S5)** — Conscious choice and living well.
- **From Understanding to Living** — bridge to the 7 Practices.

> The canonical text for each module lives in `mok/content/learn/`.

## Delivery Formats

- **Text** — fully accessible, downloadable.
- **Audio** — narrator voiceover.
- **Short video** — 2–3 minute animated summaries.

## Engagement Model

- **Progressive unlock** — each S unlocks after substantial engagement with the previous.
- **30-minute daily ceiling** on Learn content.
- Re-engagement allowed with no completion pressure.

## Anti-Patterns

- No quizzes, no knowledge checks, no completion scores.
- No notifications encouraging "finish Learn."
