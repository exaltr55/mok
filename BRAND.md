# Brand & Naming Canon

> Single source of truth for how Mokshly, YouSourceful, and future products
> are named in product, marketing, and code. If you have a naming question,
> answer it from here. If this file doesn't answer it, update this file.

---

## The hierarchy

We use an **endorsed-brand** model — Mokshly is the corp signature; YouSourceful
is the product practitioners use; future products will get their own ownable
names and remain endorsed by Mokshly.

| Level | Name | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporation** | **Mokshly** | Legal pages, careers, About, contracts, the "by Mokshly" endorsement tag, donor/grant materials, enterprise sales decks. |
| **Tagline** | **Be YouSourceful** | Wordmark imperative — the three-line logo: `be / YouSourceful / by Mokshly`. |
| **Product** | **YouSourceful** | The app title, the marketing site, employee comms, every screen a practitioner ever sees. |
| **Sections inside YouSourceful** | **Today · Practice · Connect · Learn · Me** | Plain single-word nouns. **No "Mokshly" prefix.** |
| **System components** | **MCI · 5S Framework · 7 Practices · AI Guide · Cohort** | Methodology / domain terms. Keep them — they describe the system, not the brand. |
| **B2B / institutional surfaces** | **Mokshly Access · Mokshly for Enterprise** | Donors, CHROs, grant officers — the corp brand is the trust signal here. |
| **Future products** | `<NewProductName>` by Mokshly | Each gets its own identity, Mokshly endorses but does not dominate. |

---

## The rule

> **If a practitioner touches it, drop "Mokshly". If an institution signs, funds, or audits it, keep "Mokshly".**

Concrete examples:

- **Inside the app**: the user sees Learn, Practice, Connect, Me — not "Mokshly Learn" or "My Mokshly".
- **Marketing site**: `yousourceful.com` (product domain) endorsed by Mokshly. `mokshly.com` is the corp homepage that lists portfolio products.
- **Enterprise sales deck**: "Mokshly for Enterprise" sells YouSourceful into corporations. The corp brand carries the trust.
- **Grant agreements**: "Mokshly Access" is the scholarship program. Donors sign with Mokshly Inc.
- **Newsletter**: **YouSourceful Weekly** (read by practitioners). The corp can publish a separate Mokshly newsletter for B2B audiences later.
- **Wordmark**: `be / YouSourceful / by Mokshly` — three lines. "Be" tagline · product · endorsement.

## Specific in-product names

| Surface | Name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App tab — daily landing | **Today** | Phase-aware daily home. |
| App tab — the 7 Practices | **Practice** | Singular noun (the activity). Routes use `/practices` (plural collection). |
| App tab — cohort + community | **Connect** | Shorthand for the cohort system + weekly Connect meeting. |
| App tab — 5S Framework + teachings | **Learn** | 5S modules plus per-practice Part A. |
| App tab — personal sanctuary | **Me** | Internal tabs: Overview · Journal · History · Reflections · Preferences. Subtitle is "your private YouSourceful sanctuary". Replaces the older "My Mokshly". |
| Consistency metric | **MCI** (Mokshly Consistency Index) | Full name used once in tooltips; everywhere else just "MCI" or "your consistency". |
| AI companion | **AI Guide** (named instance: Anjali / Marcus / Priya / David) | Generic role; per-user instance has a human name. |
| Cohort weekly meeting | **Weekly Connect** | The 15-minute meeting. The tab is "Connect"; the meeting is "Weekly Connect". |

## Things to *not* say in user-facing copy

- ❌ "Mokshly Learn", "Mokshly Do", "Mokshly Connect", "Mokshly Practice" → ✅ Learn / Practice / Connect
- ❌ "My Mokshly" → ✅ Me (or "your YouSourceful sanctuary" in long form)
- ❌ "the Mokshly app" → ✅ YouSourceful, or "your practice"
- ❌ "Mokshly Weekly" → ✅ YouSourceful Weekly
- ❌ Mixing "the YouSourceful platform" and "the Mokshly platform" in the same paragraph

## Things to *still* say (institutional surfaces)

- ✅ Mokshly Access (scholarship program — donor-facing)
- ✅ Mokshly for Enterprise (sales narrative for CHROs)
- ✅ Mokshly Admin (internal platform admin console for the Mokshly team)
- ✅ Mokshly Inc. (legal entity in contracts, footer copyright)
- ✅ "by Mokshly" (endorsement tag in the wordmark and marketing footer)

## Future products

When Mokshly launches its next product, it gets its own ownable name and lives
in the portfolio at the same level as YouSourceful:

```
Mokshly (corp)
├── YouSourceful — Human Sustainability system
├── <Product 2> by Mokshly
└── <Product 3> by Mokshly
```

Avoid the trap of "Mokshly Coach", "Mokshly Foundry" etc. — that's a
branded-house pattern that dilutes each product. Each new product earns its
own name.

## Voice & tone

The Mokshly voice is calm, observational, and editorial. It speaks in
sentences, not slogans. It avoids hype, exclamation marks, and engagement
prompts. When in doubt:

- *"You returned today."* not *"Way to go!"*
- *"The practice is waiting."* not *"Don't lose your streak!"*
- *"This is your record alone."* not *"See how you stack up!"*
