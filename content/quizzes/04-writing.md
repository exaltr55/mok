# Quiz — I M Writing

## When's the best time for I M Writing?
End of day, before sleep. The mind unloads onto the page so rest comes easier — and the day gets a quiet closing.

## Does this need to read like a novel?
Not at all. Grammar isn't the point. Beauty isn't the point. Honesty is. This page is yours alone — no one's grading it.

## What does it slowly grow?
Self-awareness. You start spotting the patterns in your own thinking — and that changes a lot of things, quietly.
