# Quiz — I M Resetting

## What is Resetting, in one breath?
A pause. A way to step back from cravings, habits, and the pull of stimulation — so you're choosing freely instead of running on autopilot.

## There's an ancient word for what we're doing here. What is it?
*Pratyahara* — the withdrawal of the senses. Not suppression, not denial. Just stepping back so the senses no longer quietly run the show.

## Is this deprivation?
Not at all. It's a holiday from compulsive habits — a small act of freedom you give yourself.
