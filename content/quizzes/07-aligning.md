# Quiz — I M Aligning

## What does Aligning bring together?
All the other practices — body, breath, thought, voice, expression, reset — into one coherent whole. Think of it as the conductor of the orchestra.

## When does this practice show up?
After the other six have become familiar. So there's actually something to align — not just an idea about it.

## What does it quietly grow?
Integrity. The felt sense of being one person across all the rooms of your life — no more code-switching just to get by.
