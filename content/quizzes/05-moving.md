# Quiz — I M Moving

## What are the three forms?
Postures, walking, and squatting. Three simple ways the body comes home to itself.

## How often, and how long?
The timing is flexible. The frequency is yours. What carries this practice forward is *consistency* — small and steady beats heroic and rare.

## What is this practice really about?
Coming home to the body. Not performance. Not perfection. Just being at home in the vessel that carries you through everything else.
