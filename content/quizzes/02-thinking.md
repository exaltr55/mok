# Quiz — I M Thinking

## What's an anchor thought?
A soft, repeating sound the mind can rest on — a home base attention comes back to when it wanders. We start with "So Hum" because it carries no meaning to chase.

## You drifted into a thought spiral. Now what?
You notice — and gently come back to the anchor. The drift isn't a mistake; the noticing *is* the practice.

## What does this slowly grow in you?
The ability to notice your thoughts instead of being swept away by them. Small superpower, practiced daily.
