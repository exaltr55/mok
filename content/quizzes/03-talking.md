# Quiz — I M Talking

## What's an affirmation, in our world?
A short, present-tense "I am" sentence — a thought you plant on purpose, instead of one that just shows up uninvited.

## Why "I am" and not "I want to be"?
Because "I am" speaks to who you're being, not what you'd like someday. That's upstream of what you do — and that's where real change actually begins.

## When do they land best?
Early, before the world starts talking back. A few quiet repetitions, with feeling, are enough. The feeling is what carries them.
