# Quiz — I M Breathing

## So, what's this 4-4-4 thing again?
A slow inhale for four counts. A gentle hold for four. A slow exhale for four. The simplest doorway back to balance — and you've always got it on you.

## When can you actually do this?
Anywhere. Before a meeting, after a hard moment, while waiting for coffee. No tools, no setup. A few conscious breaths and you're back.

## And quietly — where does it bring you?
Back to the Source. The Awareness in which every experience arises. The breath is the bridge.
