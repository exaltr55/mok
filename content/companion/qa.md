## What is the 5S framework?

The 5S framework names the five layers through which every life experience takes shape:

- **Source** — the Awareness in which everything arises.
- **Seed** — the thought-pattern that begins to form.
- **Soil** — the conditions that meet that pattern.
- **Season** — the cycle of time through which it unfolds.
- **Sowing** — conscious participation in what we grow.

You can read each one in Learn → Part 1.

## What is So Hum?

So Hum is a soft mental sound used as the anchor in the I M Thinking practice. "So" lands on the inhale, "Hum" on the exhale. It carries no meaning to follow — just a quiet pattern the mind can rest on. If a different anchor fits you better, change it on the Anchor settings page.

## Can I change my anchor thought?

Yes. From the I M Thinking session screen, tap "Change anchor thought." You can type anything that feels right — a word, a short phrase, something familiar or meaningful to you. Multi-word anchors cycle word-by-word with each breath; multi-line anchors land one line at a time, at the pace you choose.

## How long should I practice?

Most practices are 3 to 10 minutes. I M Thinking has options for 5, 10, and 20. There's no minimum to make it count — even a few conscious breaths in your day matter. Consistency carries more than length.

## When should I practice?

Whenever you can return to it. The morning before the day takes hold is a natural place to start. Many practitioners use small pauses through the day too — before a meeting, after a hard conversation, while waiting. Your Rhythm page holds your committed time.

## What is the I M Breathing practice?

A simple 4-4-4 rhythm — inhale, hold, exhale, each for a count of four. Done three times in a session. The breath is one of the simplest ways to return to the Source — always available, no tools needed.

## What is the I M Thinking practice?

A gentle observation of thoughts using a single anchor thought (So Hum by default). When attention drifts, you return — not to suppress thoughts, but to see them. Over time, the relationship to thinking softens.

## What is the I M Talking practice?

Seven core affirmations spoken to yourself with intention. "I am caring for my health…", "I am welcoming abundance…" and so on. The voice within shapes how you experience everything else.

## What is the I M Writing practice?

A short journal entry — expressive, reflective, or gratitude. One entry per day. Your words stay private. You can revisit and edit today's entry as many times as you like before midnight.

## What is the I M Moving practice?

Conscious movement of the body — small attention to how you sit, stand, walk, and stretch. The body is part of the practice, not separate from it.

## What is the I M Resetting practice?

Conscious pauses from food and from screens — a daily mini-reset and a deeper weekly one. The body and mind use the pause to repair and clear.

## What is the I M Aligning practice?

The final practice. Living in greater harmony with your nature, your work, and your relationships. Less about doing more — more about choosing in alignment with who you actually are.

## How does the journal work?

Open Journal → write today's entry — choose a style (expressive, reflective, or gratitude) and write freely. One entry per day. You can edit it any time before midnight. Saving the entry also logs the I M Writing practice for the day.

## What are the phases?

Practitioners move through four phases as the practice deepens:

- **Arriving** (weeks 1–7) — meeting yourself for the first time.
- **Steadying** (weeks 8–10) — the practice begins to hold.
- **Integrating** (weeks 11–12) — the framework starts to live in daily life.
- **Living** (week 13+) — practice is no longer separate from life.

Your current phase is shown on the Rhythm page.

## What is a cohort?

A small group of practitioners moving through YouSourceful together — fifteen minutes a week, listening and being heard. Optional. The option will appear on your Rhythm page when Connect opens for you.

## How do I set my schedule?

Open Me → Rhythm. Pick your days per week, time of day, and (optionally) a specific clock time. You can also set your cohort meeting day there.

## Why So Hum and not another anchor?

So Hum aligns naturally with the rhythm of the breath — "So" on inhale, "Hum" on exhale. It carries no meaning to follow, which makes it a clean place for attention to rest. It's the default, not the only choice. Use what feels natural to you.

## Is the journal private?

Yes. Your journal entries are visible only to you. No one else — not your cohort, not your employer, not Buddy. Journal content is never sent to any system outside the app.

## What if I miss a day?

Nothing happens. Streaks aren't tracked aggressively, and there's no penalty. Return when you can. The practice has been waiting all along.

## Can I do practices in any order?

Yes. The curriculum suggests a path — I M Breathing first, then Thinking, Talking, Writing, Moving, Resetting, Aligning. But once you've read each practice's "what" and "how," you can return to any of them as often as feels right.
