# The 7 Practices

You have just walked  
through the 5S Framework —  
the way every life experience  
takes shape.

Now begins Part 2  
of the YouSourceful system —  
the 7 Practices.

Each one is a small,  
repeatable act of returning.

Together, they translate the 5S Framework  
into something you can live.

## Where they fit

The 5S Framework gave you  
a way to see.

The 7 Practices give you  
a way to be —  
to meet yourself,  
breath by breath, day after day.

Each practice is short.  
Most take under ten minutes.

Three to begin the day, in one sitting.  
One at the end of the day, before sleep.  
Three more, introduced gradually as your rhythm with the first four takes hold.

## What you'll meet

**I M Breathing**  
returning to Source through the breath.

**I M Thinking**  
accessing the Source through an anchor thought.

**I M Talking**  
shaping new patterns through affirmations.

**I M Writing**  
releasing what's inside onto a page.

**I M Moving**  
consciously moving the body for balance.

**I M Resetting**  
taking conscious pauses from food and screens.

**I M Aligning**  
bringing it all together.

## How they unfold

You won't meet all seven at once.

In the first three weeks,  
you'll work with the first four —  
the foundation.

Then one new practice arrives  
each week, gently —  
*Moving* in week four,  
*Resetting* in week five,  
*Aligning* in week seven.

This pacing is deliberate.  
A new practice is offered only  
when the previous ones  
have had time to settle.

## How to read each one

Each practice has two short readings.

Part A — the what.  
Part B — the how.

Read them in order.  
Then the guided session opens.

There is no rush.  
A few minutes a day is enough.

## Where to start

We begin with I M Breathing —  
the simplest doorway,  
always available.

When you're ready,  
we'll meet you there.
